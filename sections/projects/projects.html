SleepAlarm

SleepAlarm is a real-time drowsiness detection web app that uses your webcam and AI computer vision to sound an alarm when your eyes stay closed too long.

Features: face tracking and Eye Aspect Ratio detection running fully in the browser (no video is uploaded), a distraction / head-turn alert, monitoring sessions with analytics, and multi-organization role-based access control with Super Admin approval.
